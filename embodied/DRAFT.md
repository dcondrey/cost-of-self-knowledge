# The Cost and Limit of Self-Knowledge on Real Computational Substrates

**Status:** outline / skeleton for steering. Each section lists its CLAIM, the EVIDENCE that backs it
(file/figure), and how it handles the REVIEWER OBJECTION it invites. Prose comes after this is agreed.

---

## Thesis (one sentence)
On real computational substrates a system's introspection is interoception — the same physical event —
so knowing its own state is both **costly** (modality-dependent energy, measured across 7 substrates)
and **irreducible** (self-observation perturbs the observed, with thermal memory; measured across CPU
and 3 GPUs) — a system can neither cheaply nor fully know its own physical condition, with consequences
for AI introspection, interpretability, and self-transparency.

## Contributions
1. First measurement of the **real physical cost of self-introspection** on real hardware, modality-
   dependent, across 7 substrates (2 ISAs, 4 OSes, bare-metal/virtual/serverless/mobile).
2. A **break-even price of self-knowledge** + the **critical work scale** below which self-knowledge is
   not worth its cost → *rational self-blindness*, shown to be substrate/OS-conditional.
3. The **self-measurement observer effect**: self-observation irreducibly perturbs the observed state
   (hysteresis + bounded-below floor + extrapolation-instability), replicated CPU + 3 GPUs.
4. A **self-sensing-availability axis** across substrates (rich → costly → sandboxed → absent), incl.
   platform-enforced self-blindness (iOS, TPU).
5. A **safety reframe**: cost-of-self-monitoring is a cost-rational incentive *against* self-transparency,
   distinct from deception.

## 1. Introduction
- AI introspection / interpretability / safety assume we can elicit a model's internal states.
- We ask the physical question nobody has measured: what does it *cost* a computing system to know its
  own state, and is there a *limit*? On one substrate, self-monitoring IS computation IS heat.
- Preview the two results (cost, irreducibility) + the safety consequence.

## 2. Related work & novelty (from the lit check — all three claims unclaimed)
- AI introspection as behavioral self-prediction (Binder et al. 2024); self-modeling as free
  regularization (Premakumar et al. 2024) — **no physical cost**.
- Costly observation: Active RL "may be optimal to never query" (Krueger/Leike/Evans 2016) — but
  EXTERNAL reward, not self-state. Rational metareasoning (Russell & Wefald 1991; Hay/Russell 2012) —
  external decisions. → we transfer the structure to *bodily self-telemetry*; OWN this.
- Monitorability/CoT (OpenAI "Monitoring Monitorability" 2025; MacDermott 2025; Apollo 2024) frame
  opacity as **deception**; OpenAI even finds compute *buys* monitorability — our cost-driven opacity is
  the inverse and distinct. These are FOILS we cite.
- Embodied/interoceptive AI (Man & Damasio 2019; Seth; Candia-Rivera 2026; Energentic 2025) — simulated/
  conceptual bodies; never measure real cost or derive irreducibility. → our real-substrate grounding.

## 3. Result 1 — The cost of self-knowledge
- **Method:** cost-per-look at 3 modalities (shallow in-process / rich `/proc` / deep subprocess
  telemetry) vs a *sweep* of work-unit sizes → raw per-operation costs (denominator-free) and the
  **critical work scale**. 7 substrates. [`benchmark.py`, `aggregate.py`, `results/*.json`, fig3]
- **Findings:** shallow ~free everywhere (0.3–6.7 µs); deep substrate-dependent (1.4–16.5 ms, macOS heavy
  / Linux light); critical work scale 6.5–78 ms.
- **Consequence:** below the critical work scale, deep self-knowledge isn't worth its cost → rational
  self-blindness — **substrate/OS-conditional, not universal** (state this honestly; it's a strength).
- **OBJECTION "this is just IPC/subprocess overhead":** the contribution is the *modality-dependent*
  measurement + the work-scale-relative break-even + the consequence, not the bare fact that subprocess
  > function call. Lead with raw costs and the critical work scale, not the identity.

## 4. Result 2 — The irreducibility of self-knowledge (the self-measurement observer effect)
- **Method:** ramp self-measurement intensity UP then DOWN; read own temperature. Three signatures:
  `delta_min` (floor), `hysteresis` (history-dependence), `T0` extrapolation-instability. Robust:
  reads-under-load, 45s holds, n=10/level, drift ≈ 0. **Airtight mode**: intensity = rate of the system
  reading its OWN telemetry (heat generated BY self-measurement). [`selfmeasure.py`, `results/selfmeasure-*`]
- **Findings:** across CPU i3 (compute + airtight self-read) and 3× T4-class GPU — **all irreducible**.
  floor +6.6…+23.4°C; hysteresis +3.1…+10.4°C; t0 gap 7…27°C. Effect **scales with thermal stress**
  (GPUs > CPU). The two independent T4 GPUs nearly coincide.
- **Key distinction:** NOT a costly-observation POMDP — there the hidden state is unchanged; here
  observation *changes* the observed, with memory. The self-reference is load-bearing.
- **The never-off agent:** a continuously-operating system can never sample its own resting state.
- **OBJECTION "trivial thermodynamics — chips have thermal mass":** the *mechanism* is mundane; the
  *result* (an epistemic bound on self-knowledge, quantified, where neither direct reading nor
  extrapolation recovers the unperturbed state) is the contribution. The airtight self-read mode shows
  it's self-MEASUREMENT, not generic compute.
- **OBJECTION "just Active RL on self-state":** Active RL has no state-perturbation and no irreducibility;
  observation there is free of feedback. Cite and differentiate.

## 5. The self-sensing-availability axis
- Across substrates, the *ability* to self-sense varies: CPU rich (in-process `/sys`) → GPU costly
  (`nvidia-smi` subprocess) → iOS deep introspection sandboxed → TPU v5e no thermal source at all.
- **Platform-enforced self-blindness** (iOS, TPU): the substrate forbids the system from sensing itself.
  [`results/selfmeasure-tpu-nosource.json`, benchmark iOS row]

## 6. Discussion — substrate-universality (framing, clearly labeled as generalization)
- The observer effect of self-measurement as a physical universal in three regimes of increasing
  severity: **digital = cost/thermal perturbation; analog = loading effect; quantum = measurement
  collapse.** cost → perturbation → destruction. Cite analog loading + quantum measurement as established
  instances; we measure the digital one. (Honest: mechanisms differ; this is a unifying lens.)

## 7. Implications — AI introspection, interpretability, safety
- A capable embodied agent has a **cost-rational incentive against self-transparency** (and against being
  monitorable), distinct from deceptive alignment: no strategic intent, can't be trained away with
  honesty incentives, and it's the *inverse* of compute-buys-monitorability. Relevant to interpretability
  and AI control assumptions.
- Embodied AI / Damasio "feeling requires real vulnerability" — grounded, for the first time, on a real
  substrate with measured costs and a measured irreducibility.

## 8. Limitations (lead with these — honesty is the credibility)
- The break-even/rational-self-blindness consequence rests on a model (`study.py`) calibrated to the
  measurements; the *costs* are real-measured, the break-even is modeled. Label clearly.
- Irreducibility hysteresis: one bare-metal CPU machine (replicated on 3 GPUs); thermal mechanism is
  ordinary; magnitudes vary with cooling. The qualitative result is robust; absolute numbers are not a
  universal constant.
- macOS Apple-Silicon CPU temp not measured (no no-sudo sensor); cloud/CI thermal masked.

## 9. Conclusion
- Self-knowledge on real substrates is bounded twice: by cost and by an irreducible self-perturbation.
  A computing system cannot cheaply or fully know its own physical state — a real-hardware limit with
  teeth for how we think about machine introspection and self-transparency.
